## About the Collection

This is a collection curated by Hugo Kaut in preparation for the University of Iowa's Conifer and Seamark String Quartet's trip to Prague in August of 2026. It seeks to highlight the lives of the prominent Czech composers:

- Antonín Dvořák
- Bedřich Smetana
- Bohuslav Martinů
